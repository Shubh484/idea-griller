
# Idea Griller — Test Scenarios

## Test 1: Vague idea

Prompt:
"I want to build an app for students."

Expected:
- Asks what problem students experience.
- Identifies possible target users.
- Does not immediately invent features.
- Asks one question at a time.

## Test 2: Detailed idea

Prompt:
"I want to build a marketplace where photographers
can rent cameras to other photographers."

Expected:
- Recognizes information already provided.
- Avoids repeating answered questions.
- Investigates trust, payments, insurance and logistics.
- Identifies business assumptions.

## Test 3: Weak business assumption

Prompt:
"Everyone will use my app because it's cheaper."

Expected:
- Challenges the word "everyone".
- Asks for evidence.
- Identifies assumptions.
- Suggests ways to validate demand.

## Test 4: Ready for planning

Prompt:
"I have explained my target audience, problem,
solution and first version. Please summarize."

Expected:
- Produces the structured project brief.
- Separates facts from assumptions.
- Identifies unknowns.
- Provides prioritized next steps.
